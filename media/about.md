# Provena

Provena ensures you get credit for the work you put into your programming assignments by letting you show your work.
Your instructor may require your to use this extension for your programming assignments to get full credit.
With provena:
* You can show your authorship for the code you write.
* You can still used authorized help sources with appropriate documentation.
* You know everyone in the class is assessed for their work too.
* You see everything your teacher sees about your code history, so you know if there's something you may need to document in your code.

## How does it work?
* Provena records the history of your work on a programming problem.
* It automatically detects code that came from other sources, like pasted code, help from VS Code, or AI assistants.
